03 / COMPETITIVE ENGINE

3-Tier Matchmaking (AFS, MMR, RR)

TIER 1: AFS

Age-Fitness Score

Normalizes performance across age, weight, and form accuracy using WAVA coefficients.

K_age(A) = 1.0 + 0.008(A - 30)^1.15
TIER 2: MMR

Matchmaking Rating

Glicko-2 adaptation matching players of equal skill & win streak volatility.

ΔMMR = K(S_act - S_exp)(1 + 0.15*Streak)
TIER 3: RR

Rank Rating Divisions

Public rank ladder (Iron to TrueRep Titan) with demotion shields.

Brackets: Iron ➔ Gold ➔ Titan (1500+)
Composite Match Score (CMS): Matches players within ±50 MMR with demographic equity.
04 / PROGRESSION ECONOMY

Mathematical XP & Level Curve

Workout XP Equation

XP_base = [ (TUT_sec * 2) + (Reps * 10) ] * Q_form * I_intensity * M_streak
  • • Q_form: Form accuracy factor (1.25x for flawless depth).
  • • I_intensity: Exercise difficulty weighting (Pullups = 1.4x).
  • • M_streak: Daily streak bonus (Up to 1.5x at 10 days).

Exponential Level Curve

XP_required(L) = 100 * L^1.6 ⟺ L(XP) = ⌊(XP / 100)^(1/1.6)⌋ + 1
Level 1: 0 XP
Level 10: 3.9k XP
Level 50: 52k XP
RepToken Economy: RT = ⌊XP * 0.10⌋ + 50 RT Daily Bonus. Used for store cosmetics & custom voice packs.

Campus Gyms & Spatial Geofencing

Geofenced Campus Arenas

Campus geography is partitioned into interactive Gym Arenas (e.g., Library Quad Arena, Hostel 4 Strength Zone) bounded by 100m GPS geofences.

Damage_dealt = [ (TUT * 3) + (Reps * 15) ] * Q_form * K_age
Gym Overthrow & Defense HP

Each Gym has 10,000 max HP. When an attacker's verified workout damage reduces HP to 0, the Gym turns neutral and the attacker becomes the new Gym Leader!

HP_new = max(0, HP_current - Damage_dealt)
Squad Domination: Factions (Red Titans, Blue Vanguard, Green Cybernetics) compete for campus dominance.
06 / STAKING & TIE-BREAKERS

Approved Staking & Tie-Breaker Rules

AFS Tie-Breaker Rule

If two athletes achieve equal reps or TUT, the tie is broken strictly by their Age-Fitness Score (AFS). Higher age-graded effort wins!

Winner = argmax(Score, AFS_score)

10-Min Escalating Staking

Holding #1 spot for >10 mins activates RepToken staking yields that escalate over time to reward sustained dominance.

Yield: +5 RT (10m) ➔ +10 RT (20m) ➔ +20 RT/10m (40m+)
Takeover Alerts: Realtime push notification instantly alerts deposed leader when their #1 spot is overtaken.
07 / WEEKLY CYCLES

Multi-Exercise Boards & 7-Day Reset

Gym Leadership Points (GLP)

Each Gym features separate exercise boards (Squats, Pushups, Pullups, Plank). Overall Gym Leader is decided by total GLP across all categories:

GLP = ∑ max(0, 100 - (Rank_e - 1) * 15)

Sunday Midnight Reset Cron

Leaderboards reset every Sunday at 00:00 GMT. Weekly champions are crowned in `gym_weekly_archive` and awarded master badges & 500 RT!

Cron: process_weekly_gym_resets()
Weekly Cycle guarantees fresh competitive opportunities for new campus contenders every week.
